Banking, one bar

One bar held at a fixed distance while the ship banks slowly from level to full left, full right and back, a roll step every 8 frames, 60 frames a second, with the Screen and pause settings above. Split jitter: on odd frames each block of bars starts 1 or 2 cycles later, so every split on every line lands 8 or 16 pixels further right, and each step blurs sideways. Per-line also swaps the jitter on alternate lines (a checkerboard in space and time), which needs a row loop that runs 62 and 64 cycles on alternate lines.

Rounded offsets (no tricks on the tilt)
Split jitter, 1 cycle (8 px) on odd frames
Split jitter, 2 cycles (16 px) on odd frames
Split jitter, 2 cycles, swapped on alternate lines
Tilt flicker (the previous idea)
All tricks: every bar line picks a flicker pair of C64 colours, with the phase swapped on alternate lines so a PAL TV's delay line blends neighbouring lines; distant and dim bars get the ghost byte (every other pixel black); the ship is an antialiased frame pair; and the tilt flickers: a segment whose exact offset falls between two lines alternates between them every frame, so the bars bank in half-line steps. One C64 frame per screen refresh, 60 a second. PAL TV runs each frame through the PAL CRT model (composite blur, delay line); sharp pixels shows the raw frames.
