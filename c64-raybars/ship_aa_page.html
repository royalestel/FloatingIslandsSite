The gull at 9 rolls × 9 pitches, from the 3D model. Each angle is a pair of sprite frames swapped every screen refresh, as the C64 swaps them every frame (50 Hz on PAL). Half-covered edge pixels light in one frame only, and pixels split between shadow and light alternate purple and light blue, in opposite phase on neighbouring lines. Fly it with the arrow keys or the pad: the sprite follows the eased roll and climb speed, as in the game.
